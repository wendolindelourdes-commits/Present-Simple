3rd Person Singular Rule: When the subject is He, She, or It, we add -s, -es, or -ies to the verb!
👩‍🏫
Add -s

Sarah (She)

"She plays guitar every day."

👨‍🍳
Add -es (-ch, -sh, -o, -x)

Chef Leo (He)

"He watches food shows."

🧑‍🎓
Consonant + y ➔ -ies

Emma (She)

"She studies English."

Negative Sentences: Use don't (I/You/We/They) or doesn't (He/She/It). The main verb goes back to base form!
🧑‍🤝‍🧑
I / You / We / They

"They don't like waking up early."

🐱
He / She / It

"The cat doesn't drink milk."

Asking Questions: Start with Do or Does. Main verb stays in base form!
Do + I/you/we/they

"Do you live near here?"

Answers: "Yes, I do." / "No, I don't."

Does + he/she/it

"Does she play soccer?"

Answers: "Yes, she does." / "No, she doesn't."
